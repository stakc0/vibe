VIBEPAD

Privacy

Effective when the Vercel integration is activated. VIBEPAD uses the following information to build, publish, and launch the projects you request.

Your builds

Your prompts, revisions, and logo descriptions are sent to OpenAI to generate your app and logo. VIBEPAD requests that these responses are not stored through the Responses API. OpenAI’s own service policies apply. Do not put private information or secrets in a project you plan to publish.

Projects and browser access

VIBEPAD uses an essential browser cookie to identify your saved projects and connections, and a short-lived cookie to protect Vercel account connections. Project snapshots, descriptions, logos, hosting details, and usage counts are stored in private Vercel Blob storage. Published project links and their source, name, description, and logo are public. Daily usage counts help enforce free generation and publishing limits.

Vercel connections

When you connect Vercel, VIBEPAD receives an installation access token, installation ID, user or team ID, and account display name. Tokens are stored encrypted on the server, separately from your public app. They are used to create and check projects and deployments, and to connect domains you request. VIBEPAD does not request access to your environment variables or billing data. Publishing sends the app’s HTML and project settings to your selected Vercel account.

Disconnecting removes the saved token and cancels pending connection attempts for this browser. It leaves deployed websites in your Vercel account. Removing VIBEPAD in Vercel’s Integrations settings revokes the provider permissions. Clearing browser cookies does not delete stored projects or revoke an integration.

Wallets and public launches

Privy provides the wallet chooser and connection service. Its SDK processes wallet connections and technical device information under Privy’s privacy policy. You choose an existing Solana wallet; VIBEPAD does not create an embedded wallet or require email sign-in.

VIBEPAD receives your public wallet address and the transaction your wallet signs for the reviewed coin launch. It does not receive your wallet’s private keys. Coin metadata, logos, website links, transaction references, and confirmed blockchain records are public. Vercel hosts project and launch records; Solana RPC services process network requests. Domain purchases take place directly with Spaceship and follow its policies.

Retention and requests

Saved tokens remain until you disconnect. Project and launch records are retained to keep published websites, token metadata, and saved launch recovery available. Blockchain records cannot be erased by VIBEPAD. Hosting providers may process operational request logs under their own policies.

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact jacknoel.e@gmail.com. Include the project or installation reference, and do not send API keys or wallet recovery phrases.

Provider policies: OpenAI, Vercel, Privy, and Spaceship.
