VIBEPAD

Your build. Your Vercel account.

Build your app and create a logo in VIBEPAD. Open Launch, then choose Connect your Vercel account. Your work stays in the original tab while Vercel opens in a separate window.

  1. Choose the Vercel account or team where you want your website hosted.
  2. Choose All Projects and approve VIBEPAD’s requested project, deployment, and domain permissions. Creating a new website requires access to new projects. VIBEPAD does not request access to your environment variables.
  3. Return to your build and choose Publish website. VIBEPAD creates a new project for this version of your app.
  4. Wait for Website published, then open the link. Your public .vercel.app address becomes the Website link attached to your Pump.fun token when you review and sign the coin launch.

Building and logos are free to visitors, subject to daily usage limits. Hosting follows your Vercel plan and its terms. Vercel’s Hobby plan is for personal, non-commercial use; commercial projects need an eligible paid plan. The .vercel.app address does not require a separate domain purchase.

Optionally buy a custom domain through Spaceship’s checkout, or connect one you already own. Add the DNS records shown in VIBEPAD and check the connection before reviewing your coin. A custom domain has separate registration and renewal costs.

Disconnect removes VIBEPAD’s saved connection for this browser. Existing websites stay in your Vercel account. To revoke VIBEPAD’s Vercel permissions, remove the integration in your Vercel Integrations settings. Reconnecting the same installation restores its website management; reinstalling with a new installation requires publishing a new website.

Keep your public website link. Browser cookies identify your saved projects and connection. Clearing cookies removes access from that browser.
