04   FAQ

Questions

What to know before your first build or launch.

What does free to build mean?

Building your website, asking for changes, and generating a logo are free, with daily generation limits. Hosting follows your own Vercel plan. A custom domain is optional and purchased separately. Your wallet pays the token launch cost shown before approval.

Do I need a wallet to start?

No. You can build and publish a website without connecting a wallet. Connect a compatible Solana wallet when you are ready to launch your token on Pump.fun.

Where does my website live?

In your own Vercel account. Connect it during publishing and VIBEPAD creates a public website with a vercel.app address. You can use that address or connect your own domain.

Does my website go on the token?

Yes. When you launch on Pump.fun, the published website address is attached to your token’s Website field. Verify a custom domain before reviewing the launch if you want to use it instead.

Can I publish without launching a coin?

Yes. Choose Publish website on the Launch page. Your website can stay online without creating a token or signing a wallet transaction.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Publish first, then connect a domain you own or buy one through Spaceship. Add the DNS records shown in VIBEPAD and use Check connection to verify it. Domain pricing and checkout are handled by your provider.

What if my build or launch is interrupted?

Keep the builder open and save a copy with Download code before leaving. Use Check publishing to check a deployment. If you already signed a token launch, check its saved status before starting another one. The docs cover these steps in detail.
